Privacy Policy — Fractal Dimension Calculator Last updated: September 30, 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how the Fractal Dimension Calculator mobile application (“the App”) handles information. The App is provided as a research and educational tool and is not a medical device and not a diagnostic tool. 1. Information the App does not collect The App does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal information. Specifically, the App: does not require user accounts or registration; does not request or access device contacts, location, microphone, camera, or file storage permissions; does not contain advertising or advertising SDKs; does not use analytics or crash-reporting services; does not transmit data over the internet — the App performs no network communication. 2. Images you select Images are chosen by you through the operating system's image picker (or your device's file browser) and are processed entirely on your device. Images never leave your device, are never uploaded anywhere, and are not retained by the App after you close it. Calculation results and any printed or PDF output are produced and kept solely under your control. 3. Printing The App offers an optional print function. Printing is handled locally by the operating system's print service to printers you choose. No print content is transmitted to the developer. 4. Children's privacy The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, and therefore no children's data is processed, stored, or shared. 5. Changes to this policy If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this address with an updated revision date. 6.
